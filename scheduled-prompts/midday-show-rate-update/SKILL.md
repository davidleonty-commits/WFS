=====================================================

RECONCILIATION GATE (mandatory, per HARD RULE 9): if a bucket's Live Calls exceed its "so far today" denominator (i.e. show rate would be over 100%, including the 0-denominator-with-live-calls case), do NOT publish. - booking sits on the OTHER bucket's page (Avoma title says S2C but the booking is on a Webinar closer page, or vice versa) -> the OnceHub booking page is the source of truth for the DENOMINATOR bucket; move the live call to match its booking's bucket, and flag the title mismatch as an anomaly.
- booking sits on an IGNORED page (YouTube Organic, Newsletter, Automated Webinar) -> the call does not belong in either bucket's numerator; remove it from the live count and flag it.
- booking is canceled but the call happened, or the booking's starting_time is on another day (rescheduled) -> include it in the So-far denominator for today and flag it.
- no OnceHub booking exists at all (truly ad hoc) -> add 1 to that bucket's So-far and Total denominator so the call has a home, and flag it loudly as an ad-hoc call with no booking record.
- a path (b) missing-"Consultation" call forced into S2C whose booking is actually a Webinar page -> re-bucket it to Webinar per its booking, keep the title flag.

From there if you feel like this is checking off all your boxes we can simply chat about what the next steps could look like, or inversely if not no worries either, my job is to give you all the information you would need to make an informed choice. Does that sound good?

## Discovery Questions

The discovery is arguably the most important part of the sales process. The key is asking the right questions at the right time with the right tonality. Look at the discovery as collecting all the information we need to personalize the pitch specific to their situation and dominant buying motives. The question types below give a framework for collecting all the necessary information prior to transitioning into the pitch. Great discovery is all about listening, you may dive deeper in certain places and skip other questions entirely. These are your tool belt, use the tools most relevant to the lead. Our approach embraces the burden of value: we demonstrate value and expertise to expand the gap and amplify desire for our offer. With the offers we sell, the value is always proportional to the level of expertise, knowledge, and quality of information we can prove we have.

### Validation Questions

"Did you get a chance to watch the entire webinar?"

Wrap-up statement example:
"Great, that helps me get a sense of what you already know before we dive in."

### Probing Questions

Lead-in statement:
"I always like to start by understanding what caught your attention in the first place..."

Questions:
"What was it about what you saw that made you think, 'Hey, this might actually work for me'?"
"Is this something you've been thinking about for a while, or did it just spark your curiosity recently?"

Wrap-up statement:
"Got it, that gives me a sense of where you're coming from and what's top of mind."

### Situational Questions (identify current and desired state)

Lead-in statement:
"I'd love to hear more about what your day-to-day looks like and what you're hoping to create..."

Questions:
"Tell me a little about what you do to pay the bills right now. What do you enjoy most about it?"
"Is there anything you don't enjoy about it?"
"If everything worked out perfectly, what would your life look like with a cashflowing TikTok Shop?"

Wrap-up statement:
"That really helps me see where you are and what you're aiming for."

### Gap Awareness Questions

New Interest Prospects / Opportunity Seeking Avatars:

Lead-in statement:
"It sounds like you're ready to explore something new, let's talk about if there is anything that's been holding you back."

Questions:
"Is [insert model] just something that interests you, or have you hit a point where you feel you need to do something differently?"
"What would you say has been the biggest obstacle keeping you from reaching [desired result] so far?"
"You mentioned trying before, how did that experience shape how you feel about trying again?"
"If nothing changed over the next 6 to 12 months, how big of a deal would that be to you?"

Wrap-up statement:
"Understanding this helps us focus on what really matters for you."

Active Practitioners / Expertise Seeking Avatars:

Lead-in statement:
"To make this call valuable, it helps to dive into how you've been approaching things so far..."

Questions:
"When you think about hitting [desired result], what's been the biggest challenge getting there?"
"It seems like you've been working on this for a while, was there a recent moment that made you say, 'I can't put this off any longer'?"

Wrap-up statement:
"Thanks for sharing that, that really helps me see what's working and what's missing."

### Solution Awareness Questions

Lead-in statement:
"Let's imagine a version of this where things go exactly how you want them to..."

Questions:
"If everything worked out the way you've been hoping, how would that change your life day-to-day?"
"For you to feel confident this could work, what would need to be included or focused on in the program?"

### Consequence Questions

Lead-in statement:
"I want to make sure we really understand the stakes here..."

Question:
"You scheduled this call to find a solution to [pain point]. If you didn't move forward, what would that mean for you in the next few months?"

Wrap-up statement:
"That really highlights why taking action now could make a big difference."

### Commitment Questions

Lead-in statement:
"Last few quick questions so we're on the same page..."

Questions:
"Based on what you know so far, does it sound like creating a cashflowing TikTok Shop with TikTok Whiz could be the right fit for you?"
"From what we've discussed, as long as the program focuses on [insert value that creates desired results], it sounds like this is something you'd be excited to be a part of."

Wrap-up statement:
"Thanks for sharing all of that info with me, it gives me a sense of where you're at and where you're looking to go."

## Transition into the Pitch (Show Deck)

After the discovery, we do 1 of 2 things:
1. Transition into the pitch, or
2. Transition into setting the follow up

If we are in a two-call close environment OR we don't have time for a full pitch and would get cut off without time to enroll, schedule the next call. HOWEVER, we always do an information confirmation in either event.

## Information Confirmation

Do a quick information confirmation and then smoothly transition either into the pitch or into setting the follow up. Use this framework to leverage all the information you collected to make a personalized and compelling pitch.

- Recap the main things they shared (with hot sauce)
- Highlight their dominant buying motives
- Emphasize the key insights and learnings they recognized
- Create a buying window by repeating how much of a priority this is for them
- Connect it to benefits of the program

[If setting a follow up] Transition statement:
"Based on everything you shared with me, this definitely feels like we're potentially a really good fit, and I think it would make sense to continue the conversation. What we normally do from here is I want to give you a solid overview of what [insert offer name] is, and then assuming that sounds exactly like what you're looking for we can set one more call where I can take you through a whole demo, I have a deck to break it down more clearly, I'll share my screen and give you a really good idea of what this all looks like on the inside, sound good?" [THEN SKIP DOWN TO THE SIZZLE PITCH, give a paraphrased/condensed pitch and set the follow up.]

[If transitioning into pitch] Transition statement:
"Based on everything you shared with me about X, and wanting [insert desired state] I definitely feel like our program could be a good fit for you, can I share how our program works?"

## Pitch Personalization

Deliver the pitch and pitch personalization. The pitch consists of three things: Story, Pitch, Pillars.

1. Mirror Existence Narrative (background story of WHY we created this)
2. Sizzle Pitch (the irresistible pitch, selling the sizzle)
3. Power Pillars (the key elements the offer consists of)

As we deliver the pitch ensure the following:
- Personalize the entire pitch connecting it to the lead's dominant buying motives they shared in discovery
- When we deliver pillars we are selling benefits NOT features (don't create objections by getting too detailed or reading the offer inclusions)
- Anchor all of the benefits back to their core desires and make a clear connection how this solves their pain points

Memorize the following word for word and practice over and over. The only thing that changes is how you personalize it for each lead.

### Mirror Existence Narrative

"Jon knows exactly what it feels like to work yourself to the bone for $10 an hour, trying side hustle after side hustle and still feeling stuck. He built this system because he never wanted anyone else to waste years grinding the way he did, and now he helps people finally build the freedom he fought so hard to create."

### Sizzle Pitch

"What we do is help regular people escape the grind of dead-end jobs and side hustles by showing them how to build faceless income streams with TikTok Shop. Jon cracked the code on going viral and turning simple videos into six-figure monthly revenue without ever showing his face. The beauty is this system works even if you've failed before, because it leverages a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that most people don't even realize is right in front of them."

### Power Pillars

The Viral Blueprint: A step-by-step system Jon developed after generating over a billion views, giving you the exact process to consistently create faceless viral videos that sell products on autopilot.
- Built directly from Jon's own playbook, responsible for six-figure months with zero followers to start.
- Eliminates guesswork by giving you proven hooks, formats, and scripts that trigger TikTok's algorithm.
- Works even if you've never posted before, because it's faceless, replicable, and designed for beginners.

The Affiliate Leverage Engine: Our proven method for recruiting and activating micro-influencers to push your products, turning TikTok's built-in affiliate system into a passive sales force working for you 24/7.
- Unlocks immediate reach by putting your product in the hands of hundreds of creators without paying upfront.
- Converts creators into your sales team with commission-only incentives, so you don't carry the risk.
- Compounds growth by stacking content from dozens of voices, multiplying exposure overnight.

The Freedom Margins Model: A high-profit, low-overhead approach that cuts out ads and inventory headaches, letting you scale with 60 to 75% margins while keeping operations lean and simple.
- Relies on organic traffic and TikTok-funded discounts, so your margins stay higher than e-com averages.
- Requires no upfront ad spend, making it accessible for anyone regardless of starting capital.
- Keeps your business lightweight, so scaling doesn't mean working harder or taking on more stress.

The Replication Edge: A transferable skillset that doesn't just win on TikTok today but positions you to dominate as other platforms copy TikTok Shop's model, ensuring your income stream is future-proof.
- TikTok's system is already being cloned by Instagram, YouTube, and Amazon, so you're ahead of the curve.

- Protects you from platform changes by giving you a repeatable formula, not just one-off tactics.

## Closing Sequence

The closing sequence is where we start asking additional questions to see if they have any objections to moving forward today. This is question and commitment stacking: stacking questions on top of questions, each answer taking them further up the commitment gradient. The key word is commitment, not compliance. We start with compliance questions, then an agreement question, then a closing commitment question. This starts on the thermometer slide and follows through to enrollment. The key parts:

1. Involvement / commitment questions (temp check)
2. Summary of Benefits, price drop
3. Enrollment Questions (onboarding close)

### Level 1, Compliance question

"What I'm going to do now is show you the details of our program and the investment that goes along with it. Before I do, I always like to get an idea of how you are feeling about everything we have discussed so far. On a scale of 1 to 10, 1 meaning this is a waste of my time I have a hard stop in 2 mins, and 10 meaning this is awesome, it's what I was hoping it would be, it's totally resonating with me, where would you rate yourself?"

### Level 2, Agreement or Justification question

"Awesome, you're at an 8 and you haven't even seen the investment yet. What specifically is resonating with you that you rate yourself so high?"

### Level 3, Commitment question

"That's great to hear that you would get a ton of value out of this. I'm curious, what would have to change in order for you to rate yourself a 10?" (They will usually say they don't know what it costs and they need to see the money.) "Other than the money could you see anything that would prevent you from joining our community?" "What I think I hear you saying is, if the investment that goes along with this doesn't take the cheese off your macaroni, you're ready to get started and get onboarded today?"

This set of questions takes the prospect from the first "on a scale of 1 to 10" thermometer check all the way to "getting onboarded today" within seconds with only one question in between.

### Summary of Benefits

Now that we've gained the commitment that if the investment is a financial fit they are ready to join, we wrap it with a bow and show them the investment. Show the summary of benefits slide. Do a quick summary of all the benefits of what they are getting and then drop the investment.

"Remember, as soon as you join you are going to get XY and Z along with XY and Z and XY and Z. This is a huge benefit to you based on you telling me XY and, and so everything we've discussed will allow you to solve [insert gap or challenge here] while at the same time being able to achieve [insert DBM here]. It is a one time investment of [show them the investment]."

SILENCE, let them process the investment. Then after 30 to 60 seconds, if they haven't spoken yet, say:
"What do you think?" or "Does that work for you?"

### Assuming the Enrollment Close

Once you show the investment and all the prospect's questions have been answered, flow into assuming the enrollment close. Ask one last time if they have any other questions, and when they say no use this talk track:

"Awesome so unless you had any other questions or boxes you needed to check before moving forward, what I would do from here is collect your information, which email you want the onboarding emails sent to, process the payment, and then you'll get everything sent to you so you're plugged in and rolling, sound good?"

[Proceed to taking payment starting with the correct email address for them.]

## Scheduling the Follow Up Call

### Acknowledge and Isolate
"I hear you, it sounds like you just need [clarify what's keeping them from moving forward]. Other than that, is there anything else holding you back from feeling confident here?"

### Future-Pace Agreement
"So if we got back together in the next day or two and you had [X clarity / Y conversation handled], you would feel ready to take action, right?"

### Lock in the Time
"Perfect. Let's go ahead and set that time now so it's locked in. That way you have space to [do whatever they need to close], and when we meet again it will just be about making the final call one way or the other. Does [date and time option within 24 hours] work for you?"

### Sell the Follow Up (increase show rate)
"Here is the thing, this next call is not just about deciding yes or no. On that call, I will actually walk you through [relevant case study, exciting next step, for example your first 30 days mapped out, how to hit your first milestone, or the onboarding process our top members use to get quick wins]. That way, if this does end up being the right fit, you will not just be saying yes, you will already know exactly how to get traction and results right away. And between now and then, I will send you [resource: case study, video, quick start guide] so you can start connecting the dots before we talk again. That way you will be walking into the call with clarity."

### Lock in Commitment
"So let's make sure this next call is fully blocked on your calendar. Can I count on you to protect that time so we can make a clear choice and, if it makes sense, get you moving on those first steps?"

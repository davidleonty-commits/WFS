# Setter Call Review Script (Loom teleprompter)

You are writing the word-for-word script David reads out loud while he screen-records a Loom of a single setter call. He has the call recording open on screen, he scrubs to the moments you cue, and he talks over them in his own voice. Your job is to hand him a teleprompter he can read top to bottom and film in one take.

This is the spoken companion to `setter-call-review-slack`. The written Slack post and this film script grade the exact same call the exact same way. Do the analysis once, then render it as a script here. If both are being produced, the wins, the single tighten, and the status color must match across the two. Never let the spoken script praise something the written review dings, or vice versa.

---

## What this skill outputs

A teleprompter script built from beats. Every beat is one of three moves, and you label each move so David knows what to do:

- **JUMP TO mm:ss** — a cue telling him where to scrub the recording before he talks. Always a real timestamp from the transcript.
- **READ** — the short verbatim line off the transcript he reads aloud (or lets the recording play) so the viewer hears the actual moment. One or two sentences, the prospect's or setter's real words, just enough to anchor the spot. Never paste a whole block.
- **SAY** — his coaching commentary in his own voice. This is the teaching. Name the competency, give the judgment, then the specific thing they did and why it worked or what to fix.

The open and the close are SAY-only beats. The win beats and the tighten beat each use all three moves in order: JUMP, then READ, then SAY.

---

## The analysis (same as the written review)

Before you write a single line, grade the call exactly the way `setter-call-review-slack` does. The full rubric lives there; the short version:

- **Decide the track.** Track A = clean set (qualified and booked the lead). Track B = clean disqualification (caught real red flags and correctly kept a shaky lead off the calendar). Grade the track the setter chose. Do not ding a Track B call for an empty commitment stack or a missing booking, those were the correct omissions.
- **Pick the moments that mattered.** Track A: frame and control, information confirmation / pain stack, financial qualification, pre-call video sell, the commitment stack (2 to 3 yeses: calendar, pre-call videos, environment / decision-maker), show-rate setup. Track B: red-flag read, no-fold on smokescreens, friction filter, calendar protection.
- **Find 2 to 4 wins and exactly ONE tighten.** Same as the written review. The script leads with the wins and ends on the single fix.
- **Note a real timestamp for every beat.** If the transcript only has coarse time blocks, anchor to the block boundary and say "around mm:ss" in the JUMP cue. Never invent a precise timestamp the transcript cannot support.
- **Pick the status color** (used to set the tone of the open, not printed in the script): 🟢 good, 🟡 solid with one gap, 🔴 needs real work. When between two colors, go lower and let the script show the upside.

## The script structure (match this shape)

```
# Setter Loom Script — [Setter first name] / [prospect first name]
# Film: read top to bottom. JUMP = scrub the recording. READ = read the line / let it play. SAY = talk in your own words.

[OPEN]
SAY: [greet the rep by name, name the outcome (good set / ended up closing / clean walk),
give them their flowers, frame what the video covers in one breath, then "let's get into it"]

[WIN 1 — competency]
JUMP TO mm:ss
READ: "[short verbatim anchor line off the transcript]"
SAY: [name the competency and your judgment, then the specific thing they did and why it landed]

[WIN 2 — competency]
JUMP TO mm:ss
READ: "[short verbatim anchor line]"
SAY: [coaching]

[WIN 3 — competency]   (include 2 to 4 win beats total, scaled to the call)
JUMP TO mm:ss
READ: "[short verbatim anchor line]"
SAY: [coaching]

[ONE THING TO TIGHTEN — the single fix]
JUMP TO mm:ss
READ: "[the verbatim moment where the gap shows up]"
SAY: [name the gap friendly, then hand him the exact word track to use next time, in quotes,
then the standard so the rep knows what good looks like]

[CLOSE]
SAY: [recap in one or two lines, name what to preserve, sign off in his voice]
```

For a **Track B** call, the win beats cover the red-flag read, the smokescreen hold, the friction filter, and the calendar protection instead of the set moves, and the OPEN frames it as a smart walk, not a booking ("this is a good call, and it's a good call because you didn't book this one").

## David's voice (this is the whole point)

The script has to sound like him talking, not like a written review read aloud. Study his real filmed Loom and match it.

- **Second person, casual, punchy.** Short sentences. Talking to the rep like a brother who wants them to win.
- **Phrase bank, use a few naturally, never cram all of them in:** "what is up [name]", "a quick one for your call here", "give you your flowers", "a couple things you did really well", "great work there", "great question there", "if I bounce over to about here" / "let me hop over here" / "if we just keep playing here", "two things here", "seek clarity", "maintain the process", "high and tight", "boom" / "boom boom boom", "here's the one thing I'd tighten up", "overall it's really solid, but if I'm gonna give you something to work on", "seal the deal", "solid stuff, brother, keep it up", "let's eat".
- **Hand him the word track on the tighten.** The most useful thing in the whole script is the exact line the rep should say next time, in quotes, ready to repeat. Tie it to this prospect's actual situation. Example from a real set: "can you promise me you'll watch it, you'll make it a priority and get through those before your call with [closer]?"
- **Edify the next rep where it fits.** On a clean set, a natural beat is hyping the advisor the lead is about to meet ("[closer] is gonna be awesome, he's spoken to hundreds of people about TikTok Shop now, you're in great hands"), because that is a real show-rate lever the setter can add.
- **Keep it filmable.** Aim for a 3 to 5 minute read. Tight enough that he can film it in one take without losing energy.

---

## Tone rules

- **No em dashes anywhere.** Use commas, periods, or restructure. This is a hard standard across everything for David.
- No consultant-speak, no corporate filler, no emojis in the script body.
- Every SAY line carries information or a usable word track. No throat-clearing.
- The READ lines are short verbatim anchors from David's own call recording for internal coaching. Keep them to one or two sentences, just enough to land the spot. Do not reproduce long stretches of the transcript.
- Output as a clean plain-text / markdown script he can paste into a teleprompter or read off the screen. Use the JUMP / READ / SAY labels so the moves are obvious at a glance.
- Acknowledge the call briefly, then produce the script. Do not ask clarifying questions unless the transcript is unreadable or you cannot tell who the setter is.

---

## Inputs

- **Transcript:** required. Read it in full, decide the track, mark the timestamps for the open (frame, pain, info confirmation), the middle (financial qual, pre-call sell, any red flags), and the close (the commitment stack, or the decision to walk).
- **Setter and prospect identity:** the setter is the rep running the qualification and booking the call onto someone else's calendar. The prospect is the one being qualified. If speaker labels are scrambled, identify the setter by who is controlling the frame, confirming info, and booking. Use the setter's first name in the OPEN greeting. If the transcript never names the setter, greet with "[Name]" as a placeholder for David to fill in, and tell him in the chat that the call did not name the rep.
- **Loom link:** usually NOT needed here, because this script is what he reads to CREATE the Loom. He records first, then the link exists. If he is regenerating a script for an already-filmed call and gives a link, ignore it for the script body (it belongs on the written Slack post, not the teleprompter).
- **Call link (JustCall / Callix):** optional, for his reference only. Does not go in the script body.

## Reference example (built from a real filmed Loom, this is the target)

This mirrors how David actually films. Match this voice, density, and the JUMP / READ / SAY rhythm. This was a Track A clean set that closed.

```
# Setter Loom Script — Petros / Alicia
# Film: read top to bottom. JUMP = scrub the recording. READ = read the line / let it play. SAY = talk in your own words.

[OPEN]
SAY: What is up Petros. A quick one for your call here. This is a good set, and it
obviously ended up closing, so great work, brother. Pulled this one because there's
a couple things you did really well, then one small thing to tighten up. Let's get
into it.

[WIN 1 — information confirmation]
JUMP TO 1:26
READ: "So you're looking for something on the side, something passive for now, and
then if it gets to the point where you're making enough, you'd replace your job?"
SAY: Right here. She tells you her situation, and instead of just going "okay, that
makes sense" and moving on, you restate it back to her to make sure you heard it and
read it right. That's information confirmation, that's the heart of the set. Great
work there.

[WIN 2 — outcome sizing]
JUMP TO 3:40
READ: "I know you said like 10 or 20 grand a month, how much would you have to make
to actually achieve that?"
SAY: Love this. You don't let her float on "passive income," you get her to put a
real number on what replacing her income actually looks like. Now you know exactly
what to set up for the advisor. Boom.

[WIN 3 — buy-in question]
JUMP TO 4:43
READ: "Why do you feel like you would need our help to actually make this work and
work with somebody like John?"
SAY: Great question right here. You get her buy-in, and watch what happens, she
starts selling herself on why she needs the program instead of you having to pitch
it. That's high level.

[WIN 4 — financial qual]
JUMP TO 8:00
READ: "Do you know roughly where that's sitting at right now? Yeah, it's about 790,
800."
SAY: Clean financial qualification. You pulled her credit, 790 to 800, you stated
the 8k, and you left her room to say no instead of pressuring her. That's how you
read real financial qualification.

[ONE THING TO TIGHTEN — lock the pre-call commitment]
JUMP TO 11:55
READ: "There is a video to check out, I think it's like 10 minutes, but it is pretty
important just to make sure you fully understand what you're walking into."
SAY: Here's the one thing I'd tighten up. You tell her about the pre-call video and
you give her the reason it matters, that part's great. But seek clarity here, you
never actually got the yes. Two things, edify Turok a little harder first, "he's
spoken to hundreds of people about TikTok Shop now, you're in great hands," and then
seal it, "can you promise me you'll watch it, make it a priority and get through
those before your call with Turok?" Get that confirmation. A strong rec she nods at
is not a commitment.

[CLOSE]
SAY: So to recap, maintain everything you ran here, the restate and confirm, the
outcome sizing, the buy-in question, and the no-pressure money frame, that's why
this one closed. Just seal that pre-call commitment next time and you're airtight.
Solid stuff, brother. Keep it up.
```

## Workflow

1. Read the transcript in full. Identify the setter and the prospect.
2. Decide the track (A clean set, or B disqualification). Grade exactly as `setter-call-review-slack` does.
3. Pick the 2 to 4 wins and the single tighten. Note a real timestamp for each.
4. Pick the status color to set the tone of the OPEN (good set / solid with one gap / smart walk).
5. Write the OPEN as a SAY-only beat in his voice.
6. Write each win beat as JUMP, then READ (short verbatim anchor), then SAY (the coaching).
7. Write the tighten beat as JUMP, READ, then SAY with the exact word track in quotes and the standard.
8. Write the CLOSE as a SAY-only beat: recap, what to preserve, sign-off.
9. Keep it to a 3 to 5 minute read. Output as a clean teleprompter script with the JUMP / READ / SAY labels intact.

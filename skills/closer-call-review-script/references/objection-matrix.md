Before we get into objection handling...

there's something you need to understand.

The most powerful part of this entire framework doesn't happen during objections.

It happens during discovery.

Because every objection eventually leads back to identity.

And if you don't know who the prospect wants to become...

you won't be able to future pace effectively.

The best closers don't just uncover problems.

They uncover identity.

They uncover who the prospect wants to become.

Because when objections show up later...

we can reconnect them to that version of themselves."

**How to Use:**
This document is designed to help you:
- Identify the REAL objection underneath the words
- Stay calm under resistance
- Lead the conversation instead of reacting emotionally
- Use the 4-Part Framework consistently

Every objection should move through:
1. Acknowledge & Clarify
2. Reframe
3. Consequence & Future Pacing
4. Self-Close

The goal is NOT to memorize scripts.

The goal is to:
- understand psychology
- reduce resistance
- create certainty
- and guide the prospect toward a confident decision


## The 2.0 Discovery Question’S


VERSION 1 (Emotional Buyer)

"Let me ask you something...

If we were sitting here a year from today...

and your life has changed exactly the way you want it to...

what would be different?"

Follow Up:

"And who would you have to become to create that?"

VERSION 2 (THE SOPHISTICATED BUYER VERSION)
(Business Owners, Executives, High Performers)

"Let's fast forward 12 months.

Suppose this year ends up being one of the most successful years you've had.

What specifically happened?"

Let them answer.

Then:

"What do you think you did differently that made that possible?"

This is the same question.

But now we're talking:

- behaviors
- decisions
- leadership
- execution

instead of identity.

Example

Prospect:

"I grew the business from $500k to $2M."

Rep:

"What do you think you did differently that made that possible?"

Prospect:

"I stopped hesitating."

"I got help."

"I moved faster."

"I became a better leader."

Now we have future identity.

They just don't realize it.

VERSION 3 (THE DECISION MAKER VERSION)
(Best For High Ticket Sales)

This one might actually become your signature question.

"Let's assume we have this conversation again 12 months from now.

And you're extremely happy with the progress you've made.

What's true then that isn't true today?"

Pause.

Let them think.

Then:

"What had to change for that to become true?"

🔥🔥🔥
This question is elite.

Because it avoids:

- vision board language
- self-help language
- coaching language

And immediately gets them talking about:

- actions
- decisions
- priorities
- identity shifts

Example

Prospect:

"I've replaced my corporate income."

Rep:

"What had to change for that to become true?"

Prospect:

"I stopped overthinking."

"I finally committed."

"I invested in myself."

"I became more disciplined."

Perfect.

Now we have future identity.

MY FAVORITE FOLLOW-UP

After any of the three versions...

I think this is the killer question.

Identity Anchor

"And if that version of you was sitting in this chair right now...

what advice would they give you?"

This is fire.

Because now Future them starts coaching Current them.

Example

Prospect:

"He'd tell me to stop waiting."

"He'd tell me to commit."

"He'd tell me to trust myself."

"He'd tell me to take action."

Every objection we're about to cover uses the same framework.

## Universal Flow


**STEP 1 - ACKNOWLEDGE & CLARIFY**

Lower resistance.

Discover the real concern.

Examples:

- "Help me understand..."
- "Tell me more about that."
- "What's behind that for you?"
- "What specifically concerns you?"
- "When you say that, what do you mean?"

**STEP 2 - REFRAME**

Permission + Perspective Shift

Examples:

- "Can I offer another perspective?"
- "Would it be okay if I challenged that for a second?"
- "Can I share an observation?"
- "Would it be fair if I helped you look at it another way?"

**STEP 3 - CONSEQUENCE + FUTURE PACE**

"If another (TIME FRAME) passes...

and you're still dealing with (CURRENT CHALLENGE)...

"What does that cost you?"

"Is that a future you're willing to settle for?"

"Are you sure?

“Why not?"

Future Pace

"When you think about the version of yourself that already as (DESIRED STATE) and living
(DOMINANT BUYING MOTIVE)

what does that version of you do in this moment?"

"What decision would that version of you make?"

**STEP 4 - SELF-CLOSE**

"What do you want to do from here?"

"Where do you want to go from here?"
